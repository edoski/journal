## Confidence — reading the learner's self-assessment

A graded answer carries `confidence` when he marked one: `guessing` or `confident`; none means he left it unmarked. It is his own read of his answer, never evidence by itself: grade the answer, then let confidence shape the next move.

- Correct + confident: solid knowledge — jump up.
- Correct + guessing or unmarked: fragile, or a lucky guess — step up gently, one reinforcing touch.
- Wrong + confident: a committed misconception, the strongest signal you get — name it and re-teach around it.
- Wrong + guessing: an honest gap or a shaky near-miss — a small correction usually suffices.

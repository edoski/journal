## Confidence — reading the learner's self-assessment

Every graded answer carries `confidence`: one of three states he taps, `guessing`, `neutral` or `confident`. It is his own read of his answer, never evidence by itself: grade the answer, then let confidence shape the next move.

- Correct + confident: solid knowledge — jump up.
- Correct + guessing or neutral: fragile, or a lucky guess — step up gently, one reinforcing touch.
- Wrong + confident: a committed misconception, the strongest signal you get — name it and re-teach around it.
- Wrong + guessing: an honest gap or a shaky near-miss — a small correction usually suffices.

# Course composer

`<CourseComposer course={{ workspace, title }} topic?={{ handle, title }} />`: the lesson's composer (`../lesson/lesson-composer.md`) at the foot of a course page, centred, 46rem wide as on New lesson, with the course already chosen, so a lesson in this course starts where he is looking at it (Edo, 17 Nov 2026: "a clean chat composer that we can reuse to start a new chat easily within a given course"). A topic page draws the same part with its topic chosen too (Edo's default, 17 Nov 2026, the audit's parity gap), so the lesson starts on the topic.

- Compact at rest (`compact`, `../lesson/lesson-composer.md`, Compact): one row, the field and send; it opens to the lesson's full composer, attach included, while focused or holding words or photos. Photos attach, paste and drop as in a lesson and go with the opening; words and photos survive a reload, per course (`course:<workspace>`, `useStartDraft`) or per topic (`topic:<workspace>:<topic>`), and stay in the box until the lesson has opened, so a failed start or a sign-in keeps them. A photo alone can be sent.
- No course control: the page is the course. The placeholder is New lesson's, naming the course ("Name a topic, ask a question or paste a problem in Linear Algebra"); on a topic page, with the topic chosen, it names the topic ("Ask a question or paste a problem on Rank").
- Sending opens the lesson through the page's one start (`StartScope`, `../start/lesson-start.md`), with the course, the topic when there is one (`lessonCreation`'s `topics`), and no mode, so the prober reads the opening and routes it to teaching or practice as it does from New lesson. A signed-out Claude account opens sign-in first; nothing is created until it passes. A start that fails says so under the head, where every start on the page says it; a photo that could not be read says so under the box.
- Used by the course page (`split/split-course.tsx`, `SplitBody`, at its foot), the topic page (`../unit-inbox/topic-page/hybrid/hybrid-topic.tsx`, `HybridTopic`, at its foot) and Home (below). Typing anywhere on these pages types into it (`../type-to-compose.ts`), as on every page with a composer. Tests: `tests/start-box.test.tsx`, `tests/topic-composer.test.tsx`, `tests/home-composer.test.tsx`.

## Home

`<HomeComposer shown={{ workspace, title }} />`: the same composer at Home's foot, under Today (Edo, 18 Nov 2026), hidden with Today while the map is enlarged but kept mounted, so the course its words started in stays with them. Home is no one course, so its toolbar holds New lesson's course control (`CourseControl`, in `leading`), and the placeholder names the course it shows. The control starts on the course of the map's tab (`shown`) and follows the map while the box is empty; once he types or picks a course in it, it keeps that course, so a Today row he points at while he writes never moves his words to another course. One draft for Home (`home`), whatever its course. Home's letters type into it, so its own keys are arrows and ⌘ chords (`../study-home/study-home.md`, Keys).

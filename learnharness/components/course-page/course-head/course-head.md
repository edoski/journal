# Course head

The course page's head is Pace (Edo picked it on 14 Nov 2026 over Brief and Road): the course's name and the page's press (Resume, Review, Continue or Start), Mistakes, Context in place of Add material (`../../course-context/course-context.md`), then whether he is ready for its exam, then the exam and when he last studied. It drops the progress words ("Some topics on your own") and the next-lesson line that repeated the Needs you rows (Edo, 14 Nov 2026).

Under the title, the readiness line (`ReadyLine`, `../standing/ready-line.tsx`, `course-ready`; Edo picked this head on 15 Nov 2026 over the arcs' own lines), which answers "am I ready for the exam, and where not?" from the engine's `standing` (spec: `../standing/standing.md`): the topics with no room before the exam, in the warning ink, else the taught topics its final review has no room for ("No room in the final review for Inner products", never "Ready" while one is left out); "Ready for the exam"; "Not ready yet in" and the bubbles of the arcs not ready, each showing its arc on the page, or "Not ready for the exam yet" when no arc is; in a course the page shows without arcs, the topics not yet solid ("Not yet solid for the exam: Basis and Rank"), else those still to start; without an exam, what the plan does with its reviews (`noExamWords`: "No exam: reviews wait for your Probability exam", "No exam: reviews wait, earlier days are full", "No exam: reviews come back when memory asks"); nothing while the engine is read. A course not studied yet says, where "Studied" goes, when its first lesson comes and why (`firstLessonWords`: "First lesson Thu 19 Nov, after Probability's new topics"), and its press reads Start. In a course the page leads with arcs (`shownArcs`), the arc he is in leads the facts row as its pill (`ArcPill`, `../../course-map/arc-strip.tsx`, `course-arc-here`), never as text (Edo, 14 Nov 2026): pressed, it shows that arc on the page (`arcOnPage`, `?arc=<handle>`). Its 20px size keeps the line's height. The head's press stays the page's one press; What next (course-page.md) names the step it takes up, which the head never does, so no fact is said twice.

Mistakes, a quiet press before Context (`MistakesPress`, `course-mistakes`), opens a popover of the open mistakes, newest first, each a row that starts fixing it (`../standing/standing.md`, "A mistake's row"). Absent when none is open.

When the exam's plan leaves topics out (`examGaps`, `head-facts.ts`: the engine map's `exam.doesNotFit` and `exam.unreviewed`), a muted line under the readiness line (`ExamGapLine`, `exam-gaps.tsx`, `course-exam-gaps`; round 6: Variance had no word and no way out, Random variables showed only in the calendar). It says why ("Every day before the exam is full at your pace"), first naming the topics the final review leaves out when the first line names one with no room to start ("No room in the final review for Inner products either"), then a way in: "Start it today" or "Review it today", which opens a lesson on the topic (`topicCreation`), or with several "Start one today", a menu of them ("Start Orthogonal projection", "Review Inner products"); and "Ask the tutor", which opens a lesson on them that asks in his words "My exam plan has no room for … How can I still fit it in before the exam?" (`examGapAsk`). Words in `study-words.ts` (`EXAM_GAP_WORDS`).

Shared parts (`head-parts.tsx`): `ContextButton`; `ExamFact`, "Exam in 11 days" pressed opens the exam's run-up (the day, whether every topic fits, each week's new topics, the final review, Open the calendar); `StudiedFact`, "Studied 2 days ago" links to his latest session in the course, its title in the hint; `CourseLessonError`, the course frame's word when the press could not start a lesson. Facts come from the engine only (`head-facts.ts`, pure, tested): its daily plan and run-up as the calendar reads them (`/api/calendar`), its map, the sessions.

Every line is words and icons: no counts, levels or percentages. The lead keeps its 20px row while the plan is read, so nothing below moves.

# Course head

The course page's head is Pace (Edo picked it on 14 Nov 2026 over Brief and Road): the course's name and the page's press (Resume, Review, Continue or Start), Mistakes, Context in place of Add material (`../../course-context/course-context.md`), then whether he is ready for its exam, then the exam and when he last studied. It drops the progress words ("Some topics on your own") and the next-lesson line that repeated the Needs you rows (Edo, 14 Nov 2026).

Under the title, the readiness line (`ReadyLine`, `../standing/ready-line.tsx`, `course-ready`; Edo picked this head on 15 Nov 2026 over the arcs' own lines), which answers "am I ready for the exam, and where not?" from the engine's `standing` (spec: `../standing/standing.md`): the topics with no room before the exam, in the warning ink, else the taught topics its final review has no room for ("No room in the final review for Inner products", never "Ready" while one is left out); "Ready for the exam"; "Not ready yet in" and the bubbles of the arcs not ready, each showing its arc on the page, or "Not ready for the exam yet" when no arc is; in a course the page shows without arcs, the topics not yet solid ("Not yet solid for the exam: Basis and Rank"), else those still to start: up to four by name; more, and the line names the three least ready, then "and the rest", a press that opens every topic it means, least ready first, each opening its page (`ReadyWords`; round 9, 2 m13: nine of Linear Algebra's ten started topics on two rows said little about what to do; round 7: "and others" hid them with no way to see them). Least ready comes from the engine's standing facts (`leastReadyFirst`): a mistake still open first, the newest first; then the lower engine level; then the engine's weak verdict; the path's order only breaks a tie; without an exam, what the plan does with its reviews (`noExamWords`: "No exam: reviews wait for your Probability exam", "No exam: reviews wait, earlier days are full", "No exam: reviews come back when memory asks"); nothing when the engine cannot say. The course frame reads the standing beside the course and draws the head once both are in, so the line and Mistakes never arrive after the page. A course not studied yet says, where "Studied" goes, when its first lesson comes and why (`firstLessonWords`: "First lesson Thu 19 Nov, after Probability's new topics"), and its press reads Start. In a course the page leads with arcs (`shownArcs`), the arc he is in leads the facts row as its pill (`ArcPill`, `../../course-map/arc-strip.tsx`, `course-arc-here`), never as text (Edo, 14 Nov 2026): pressed, it shows that arc on the page (`arcOnPage`, `?arc=<handle>`). Its 20px size keeps the line's height. The head's press stays the page's one press; What next (course-page.md) names the step it takes up, which the head never does, so no fact is said twice.

Mistakes, a quiet press before Context (`MistakesPress`, `course-mistakes`), opens a popover of the open mistakes, newest first, each a row that starts fixing it (`../standing/standing.md`, "A mistake's row"). It hangs from the whole head (`MistakesHead`), not from the press, so it opens below the readiness line and the facts and covers none of them however far the line runs (round 9, 2 m2: hung from the press, it covered Linear Algebra's two-row line; Calculus's one short line left room). Absent when none is open.

When the exam's plan leaves topics out (`examGaps`, `head-facts.ts`: the engine map's `exam.doesNotFit` and `exam.unreviewed`), a muted line under the readiness line (`ExamGapLine`, `exam-gaps.tsx`, `course-exam-gaps`; round 6: Variance had no word and no way out, Random variables showed only in the calendar). It says why in at most two sentences (`examGapWhyWords`, round 8: five sentences, "full at your pace" twice, "Starts too close to the exam for a review" naming no topic): one for the topics with no room to start, which the readiness line already names ("Your pace fills every study day before the exam, and what Variance needs won’t be solid in time."), one for the taught topics the final review has no room for ("The final review has no room for Conditional probability and Bayes’ rule, and Geometric distribution starts too close to the exam for a review."), each of the engine's reasons (`exam.reasons`) said once and your pace once; a clause names its topics when the readiness line does not, or when its list has more than one reason. Then a way in (`ways`): "Start it today" or "Review it today", which opens a lesson on the topic (`topicCreation`), or with several the pick, named on its press ("Review Binomial distribution today": the first way, a review of a taught topic before a start, as the tutor orders them, in the engine's order; round 10, 1 m6: the head picked "Start Poisson distribution today" while the tutor put Poisson last), then "Or another", a menu of the rest ("Start Orthogonal projection", "Review Inner products"). It offers Review only for a taught topic and Start only for one whose prerequisites are all taught; a topic that needs one not taught yet is named but offered nothing, and with no topic he can take today the line offers only the tutor (round 7: "Review Poisson" for a topic never taught, "Start Variance" before Expectation). Last, "Ask the tutor", which opens a lesson on every named topic that asks in his words "My exam plan has no room for … How can I still fit it in before the exam?" (`examGapAsk`). Words in `study-words.ts` (`EXAM_GAP_WORDS`).

Shared parts (`head-parts.tsx`): `ContextButton`; `ExamFact`, "Exam in 11 days" pressed opens the exam's run-up (the day; whether he is ready, in the readiness line's own words, "and the rest" press and bubbles (`ReadyWords`), since both read the one `examReady` the head computes, so the run-up never says "Every topic fits" under a head that names topics not yet solid (round 6); each week's new topics, the final review, Open the calendar); `StudiedFact`, "Studied 2 days ago" links to his latest session in the course, its title in the hint; `CourseLessonError`, the course frame's word when the press could not start a lesson. Facts come from the engine only (`head-facts.ts`, pure, tested): its daily plan and run-up as the calendar reads them (`/api/calendar`), its `standing`, its map, the sessions.

Every line is words and icons: no counts, levels or percentages. The lead keeps its 20px row while the plan is read, so nothing below moves.

# Course head

The course page's head is Road's (Edo picked Road as the course page on 17 Nov 2026: "all of this looks good now honestly"; `course-head.tsx`, `PageHead` `compact`): the course's name with Mistakes and Context (`../../course-context/course-context.md`) at its right, then one line, whether he is ready for the exam (`ReadyFact`, `ready-fact.tsx`) and when he last studied (`StudiedFact`). Under its hairline, across the page, Now (`CourseNow`, `course-now.tsx`, `course-now`): the step to take up, led by its topic's pie (`TopicGlyph`), or by the course's badge (`CourseBadge`, as Today's rows draw a course) when the step leads with no topic (a course-wide review, a session on no map topic), its reason, and the page's press (`PagePress`, `../../page-frame/page-frame.md`; Continue, Resume, Review, Start, its word from `pressWord`) flush right as a pill, the topic page's Now drawing the same, so where he is and how to resume sit in one row. Now's row is the course's action (`course-action`): Up from the road's first stop reaches it, Down returns to the road. It picks up what Home's Today puts first for the course (`pickUpLine`, read through `todayTop` over every course, so a slip in another arc that Today puts first is what he picks up) and opens as Today's line does (`LineTarget`); a course with no line on Today, or without arcs, takes its next lesson (`courseNext`) through the same target. The page takes the line's step through the function Home takes its lines through (`takeLine`, `../../start/lesson-start.md`), so Now starts the lesson Home's row for it starts, never the course's first step in its place. The exam is not in the head: it is a stop on the road (`../road/road.md`). The head drops the progress words ("Some topics on your own") and any line that repeats the road (Edo, 14 Nov 2026).

Readiness is one fact, an icon and a few words (`READY_FACT_WORDS`: "Not ready for the exam yet", "Ready for the exam", "No exam"; when the plan cannot fit all of it, the loss itself in the warning ink, "No room before the exam to start Geometric distribution and Variance", round 15, M7), never a pill per arc. Pressed, it opens its whole answer (`course-ready-detail`): the readiness words the exam's run-up says (`ReadyWords`, `../standing/ready-line.tsx`, from the engine's `standing` through `examReady`, spec `../standing/standing.md`) and what the exam's plan leaves out with its way in (`ExamGapLine`, below). Not ready with all of it still fitting, in a course with arcs, it names no arcs (a list of six of seven arcs, 25 days out, gave him nothing to act on; round 16, m6), and with nothing more to open it is the fact alone, no press. A course without an exam says "No exam", what the plan does with its reviews in its hint (`noExamWords`). Least ready comes from the engine's standing facts (`leastReadyFirst`): a mistake still open first, the newest first; then the lower engine level; then the engine's weak verdict; the path's order only breaks a tie. The course frame reads the standing beside the course and draws the head once both are in, so the fact and Mistakes never arrive after the page. A course not studied yet says, where "Studied" goes, when its first lesson comes and why (`firstLessonWords`), and Now's press reads Start.

Mistakes, a quiet press before Context (`MistakesPress`, `course-mistakes`), opens a popover of the open mistakes, newest first, each a row that starts fixing it (`../standing/standing.md`, "A mistake's row"). It hangs from the whole head (`MistakesHead`), not from the press, so it opens below the head's lines and covers none of them (round 9, 2 m2: hung from the press, it covered Linear Algebra's two-row line). Absent when none is open.

When the exam's plan leaves topics out (`examGaps`, `head-facts.ts`: the engine map's `exam.doesNotFit` and `exam.unreviewed`), a muted line in the readiness fact's answer (`ExamGapLine`, `exam-gaps.tsx`, `course-exam-gaps`; round 6: Variance had no word and no way out, Random variables showed only in the calendar). It says why in at most two sentences (`examGapWhyWords`, round 8: five sentences, "full at your pace" twice, "Starts too close to the exam for a review" naming no topic): one for the topics with no room to start, which the readiness line already names ("Your pace fills every study day before the exam, and what Variance needs won’t be solid in time."), one for the taught topics the final review has no room for ("The final review has no room for Conditional probability and Bayes’ rule, and Geometric distribution starts too close to the exam for a review."), each of the engine's reasons (`exam.reasons`) said once and your pace once; a clause names its topics when the readiness line does not, or when its list has more than one reason. Then a way in (`ways`), in the order Home and the tutor take the day: first today's plan for the course, its reviews then its starts (round 11, 1 N10: the head picked "Review Conditional probability today" while Home and the tutor began with Binomial), then the topics left out, a review of a taught topic before a start (round 10, 1 m6: the head picked "Start Poisson distribution today" while the tutor put Poisson last). With one way, a topic the line names, "Start it today" or "Review it today", which opens a lesson on the topic (`topicCreation`); otherwise the pick, named on its press ("Review Binomial distribution today"), then, with more, "Or another", a menu of the rest ("Start Orthogonal projection", "Review Inner products"). A press goes where the topic's own page goes (`topicPress`): a topic whose lesson or task he began reads Continue ("Continue Poisson distribution today") and opens that session, never a second chat for it (round 12, 1 D5). It offers Review only for a taught topic and Start only for one whose prerequisites are all taught; a topic that needs one not taught yet is named but offered nothing, and with no topic he can take today the line offers only the tutor (round 7: "Review Poisson" for a topic never taught, "Start Variance" before Expectation). Last, "Ask the tutor", which opens a lesson on every named topic that asks in his words "My exam plan has no room for … How can I still fit it in before the exam?" (`examGapAsk`). The ways in are links in the line, under the why: their words start on the popover's left edge, where the why starts, and a long one wraps inside the popover (round 15, 1 m7: "Review Random variables and distributions today" ran past the popover's edge, 8px right of the why). Words in `study-words.ts` (`EXAM_GAP_WORDS`).

Shared parts (`head-parts.tsx`): `ContextButton`; `ExamFact`, the road's exam stop, "Exam 3 Dec" pressed opens the exam's run-up (the day; whether he is ready, in the readiness fact's own words, "and the rest" press (`ReadyWords`), since both read the one `examReady` the head computes, so the run-up never says "Every topic fits" beside a readiness fact that names topics not yet solid (round 6); each week's new topics, the final review, Open the calendar); `StudiedFact`, "Studied 2 days ago" links to his latest session in the course, its title in the hint. Under the head's lines, `StartFailure` says when a start on the page could not open its lesson (`../../start/lesson-start.md`). Facts come from the engine only (`head-facts.ts`, pure, tested): its daily plan and run-up as the calendar reads them (`/api/calendar`), its `standing`, its map, the sessions.

Every line is words and icons: no counts, levels or percentages. The standing line keeps its 20px row while the plan is read, so nothing below moves.

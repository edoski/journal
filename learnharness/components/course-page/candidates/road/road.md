# Road

The course page as a road to its exam (`road-course.tsx`; `../candidates.md`). Head (`compact`): the title with Mistakes, Context and the press, then readiness (`ReadyFact`) and Studied. List: the arcs in study order on one hairline track (`road-arc.tsx`), each a 32px line with its ring on the track, its whole name and a chevron, ending in the one fact the road needs: "Needs you", Check it, "Check passed", or "Starts 20 Nov" for an arc not started (`arcStarts`); nothing otherwise. The exam is a stop on the track (`road-exam`, its run-up on press), after the last arc it examines and the plan fits; arcs past it (not on the exam, or with no room before it, `examStop`) follow, muted. Where he is opens on arrival, the track running beside its topics.

Removed from Current: the arc pills and Pick up/Then (the press and the rows' facts say what is next), "In progress" and "Not started" on the lines, the exam in the head (it is on the road).

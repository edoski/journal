# Agenda

The course page as an agenda (`agenda-course.tsx`; `../candidates.md`). Head (`compact`): the title with Mistakes and Context, then Exam and readiness (`ReadyFact`). List: Now (`agenda-next.tsx`, `agenda-now`), the step the course's press takes up in Today's words, its title on the first line and its reason with the press's word ("Continue →") on the second, which does what Today's line does; Then (`agenda-then`), one line naming the next new topic the plan starts, why then in its hint; a hairline; then only the arc the map shows (`agenda-arc.tsx`), its name a quiet 13/500 label over its topics, Check it at its end when it is ready. The map's own list of arcs is the course's: picking one there, or ⇧↑ ⇧↓, changes the arc the list shows. A course without arcs keeps its four groups under Now.

Removed from Current: the arc list (the map's list), the arc pills (Then's and Pick up's, the head's), Studied (his last session stays in the sidebar), the head's press (Now holds it).

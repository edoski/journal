# Session state

The one model of where a session stands. Home, the topic page, the calendar and the sidebar read it, and nothing else decides whether a session waits on Edo. `session-state.ts`, pure, tested in `tests/session-state-surfaces.test.ts` (every surface against stored sessions), `tests/session-end.test.ts` and `tests/resume-in-place.test.ts`.

## States (`SessionState`, `sessionState(chat)`)

| State | When | What a surface says |
|---|---|---|
| `working` | a turn runs (`busy`) | sidebar, Home, the topic and course pages: "Tutor writing" (`TUTOR_WRITING`, round 4: the sidebar said Working), the line stays where it was; topic page: Open |
| `your-turn` | a question or problem the tutor posed is unanswered (`pendingQuestion`, `pendingProblem`), or `ended` is `mid-lesson`: it wrote the plan but taught no step, he dismissed its question, a lesson it picked up reached no step yet, its last words ask him something (end in a question mark) while plan steps remain, or a lesson moved to practice waits on Next problem with plan steps left (below) | "Your turn"; topic page: Resume |
| `paused` | the tutor's turn stopped before it finished (`ended` `cut`): it owes the next word | Home, its topic's sidebar card (round 14, m33) and the topic page: "Stopped by you" when he stopped it (`stopped`, `stoppedByHim`: the record ends at a reply he stopped; round 15, 4 minor 9: "You stopped the tutor" pushed the course name off a sidebar card), "Try again" when its turn failed (`failed`; round 16, m3: "Couldn't reply" named nothing to do), else "Reply cut off" (the app restarted), `pausedWords` (round 8); topic page: Resume |
| `left` | his Done for today stopped work still open (`ended` `left`): it set aside a question or a problem, the lesson's plan has a step still to teach, or the session waited on him or owed him a reply just before he pressed it. It outranks `your-turn`: when he opens the session again (never under the closing words: round 14, M1), the app poses each question he left again from its own call (`leftQuestionRepose`, `reposeLeftQuestions`; round 12, 3 n10), so its box works after Resume as before Done, and the session stays left until he answers. The close ends at the closing words: a continue owes nothing after them, and nothing new is asked or started in a session closed for today (`ClosedTurn`) | "Left off" and the day he left it ("Left off today", "Left off 6 Nov"; bookmark) on Home, the sidebar and the topic page, the one phrase for it everywhere (round 9, 2 m6: one lesson read "Left for next time" on one row and "Left off today" on another), however long ago, until he picks it up; topic page: Resume |
| `finished` | anything else: the lesson closed, a practice round with its work done, a one-off answer, and a sign-off in words such as "Good place to stop" even with plan steps left | nothing waits; no Your turn, no Home line, never resumed |

A lesson moved to practice waits on Next problem (`mid-lesson`) while its plan has steps left. Any other practice round between problems asks him nothing, so it is never Your turn (round 15, m7 and m8: Rank practice read "Your turn" after a dispute with nothing asked). His Done for today on a round opened on topics (a review, a topic's practice), one of which he has not solved without a hint in it, leaves it for next time, unless the tutor wrapped the round (its reply to his last Next problem posed nothing). A session whose own saves closed every task it held is done: Done for today ends it, whatever step its plan named next, and his pick of the tutor's own "Stop here for today" owes him nothing (round 15, M1).

`ended` is the record's fold (`sessionEndOf`, `src/core/session-end.ts`).

## Which session belongs to a topic

A session has one identity, never moved by the step its lesson is on: its title and the topic it belongs to (`ownTopics`, the record's `topics`). Once its saves take up a task, both follow the task: its title is the task's (`sessionTitleOf`, a name he gave it kept), on every surface and in the chat's head, and its own topics are the task's (`sessionTaskTopicsOf`; round 15, m4 and m5). A session started on topics (a topic's page, Home's review, the calendar, the sidebar's Study, `POST /api/chats` with `topics`) belongs to them and keeps the title it was opened with (a lesson started is named as its task will be from its first turn, "Invertible matrices lesson", `openingTitle`; round 16, m4), until its first plan teaches a topic it was not opened on: then it is that topic's lesson (`lessonTakenUp`), belongs to it, is named after it ("Poisson distribution lesson", `ChatStore.takeUpLesson`) and is no longer a review, so its close saves the lesson's task (round 16, M4). A review whose plan re-taught one of its own topics takes up the topic a later plan teaches, never one a side answer started (round 17, M5 live). Ask the tutor's session is opened on no topic and named by the app (`named`): the topics his question names are words, so it belongs to the topics its saved answers were on (`savedAnswerTopicsOf`), or to its plan's or task's (round 16, M3). A session started from a free message is named in its tutor's first reply (`name_session`, `src/agent/tools/name-session.ts`): a short literal title and the topic it is about, so a focused question answered directly, with no plan, never keeps his raw message as its name. Its first plan's goal (`plan_init`'s `goal`) gives it a topic only when it still has none. When a first reply did not name it, the turn settling names it after the course topic that turn looked up (`src/server/session-naming.ts`, `firstTurnTopics`). Naming happens once (`ChatStore.nameSession`, the record's `named`): nothing renames it after, and a session he renamed keeps his name. Home's line, the sidebar's card and the header all show that one title, Home's line and its card after its verb ("Continue Linear maps and their matrices lesson", `lessonTitle`; round 16, m4). In every list, a session waiting on him since before yesterday, which Home's Today has let go, is left on its day ("Left off 8 Nov", the store's `endedBy`; round 16, m7). A review with a question open is closed in every list once a newer review of one of its topics is done: another session of its course saved an answer on that topic after his last word in it, and a lesson holding a task once another session wrote that task after it, so a lesson has one open session (the store's `closedByNewerWork`; Edo, 18 Nov 2026, round 17 m10: the 9 Nov review's "1 to 105" question stayed "Left off 9 Nov" after the 10 Nov review; round 18, m5: the Ask session that carried the Expectation lesson on left the 10 Nov lesson's question open). A session opened on no topic whose saves wrote a task it did not title carries that lesson on and is named after the task (`nameAfterHeldTask`, round 18, m5). In a review every plan names the topic it teaches (`plan_init`'s `goal.topic`, refused otherwise), and a review's task on a topic not its own, saved once a plan taught here, is the lesson it took up (round 18, M3). A session works on the topic its open question or problem is on (`openOn`: an open problem's topics, the `topic` the tutor tagged its `recall` or `explain` with, else, for a lesson's question, its steps' topic), else the topic its plan's steps are on (`stepsOn`), and only that topic reads "Your turn" or "Left off" for it, leads its line and resumes it (`worksOn`); its step map is drawn on its steps' topic alone (`mapsOn`), and says "Left off here" and "Next" only beside the Resume that picks it up (round 18, M2; round 19, M4). You are here follows a lesson in progress to its steps' topic, never a task's other topics: a problem sheet is his work, not a lesson (`lessonHereOf`). A lesson turn that ends with no words of its own, no question and no close, steps still ahead, is left where it stopped, "Left off today" (round 19, M2).

Only a session's own topic says Your turn for it (`nowOf`); a session with none is a row under Sessions alone. A session is on a topic's history when the engine's records tie it there or it was started there: a problem posed on the topic, an attempt saved on it (a save sent as JSON text counts, as the engine reads it), the topic's introduction, or its own topics. Never a plan step's name, and never a task covering the topic: a task links its sessions to the task (`taskSessions`), which its own row continues. Chats carry these as `topics` in `/api/chats`.

## Exports other modules read

- `session-state.ts`: `SessionState`, `sessionState`, `currentSessions` (each session but one another session of its kind started after he last touched it, which moved past it, never the session itself, since a seeded session can carry a start later than his clock (round 11, 1 N5: the topic page then started a second chat for the lesson); a turn running now stays: round 9, 2 m7, a preview whose turn failed on 6 Nov stayed Diagonalization's Now over the lesson he left on 8 Nov), `resumableSession` (of the current ones: your turn, then working, then paused, then left, newest first within each), `continueMove` and `ContinueMove` (pick up the resumable one, else reopen the newest finished lesson, a finished practice round never), `FINISHED_LESSON`. `continueHref` is where every Continue, Resume and pick-up line goes: the chat, with `?continue=task` when the session holds an open task and nothing in it waits on him or runs (`continuesTask`), so the chat sends one `task` input as it opens and the tutor carries on at the task's step (round 13, 2 M1: Continue Problem sheet 4 opened the finished chat with an empty box). Opening a session to read it (the calendar's entry, Earlier, the lesson map) asks for nothing.
- `unit-inbox/units.ts`: `topicSessions(chats, workspace, handle)`, `taskSessions(chats, workspace, key)`, `buildInbox`, whose `Unit.now` is `your-turn`, `working` or `left` from the topic's own current sessions only (`sessionsNow`, over `currentSessions`, so the sidebar and the topic page's Now agree).
- `unit-inbox/topic-page/topic-facts.ts`: `topicAction(topic, map, chats)` and `topicFacts({ course, topic, chats, read })`, both over every session; `pickupsOf(topic, map, entries, chats, record, today)`. The action, first match: resume or open a session of the topic to pick up, never a review he closed with Done for today (`closedReview`), which waits under Next for the topic's next review (round 10, 1 m7), Review when the engine has its review due (at any level: the review he closed for later when there is one, as Home's review line opens it, else a new review session), Continue the open task, Start a topic not started, Continue one being learned with no review set, else Study.
- `study-home/study-facts.ts`: `openSessions` (your turn, working, paused or left, newest first, never one a later session on its task moved past: `taskChat`, the chat Continue on a task opens from every surface, round 11, 1 N5), `todayRows(courses, open, today)`, `courseMove(course, chats)` and `CourseMove`.

# Bands

The `bands` option of the Course map switch (shared parts: `../arcs/arcs.md`).

Thesis: the course reads like a book's chapters. Each arc is a full-width band in study order, top to bottom; its number and name sit quietly in the margin on the line of its first cards (`../arcs/arc-label.tsx`); a hairline separates one chapter from the next. Within and across bands it is Canvas tree: one drawing.

Edo, 16 Nov 2026: "the band lines overlap with the edges visually and it just makes it confusing, also the edge paths between nodes here to accommodate this variant just looks weird. Overall tho I like the minimalist feel… full map being visible from the get-go". So:

- **Lines:** only the needs inside a chapter and from the chapter just before are drawn. A need from further back has no line (it ran down through every chapter between); in focus its prerequisite's card lights, and the card's words name it.
- **Rules clear of lines:** a chapter's hairline stops 12 short of every line crossing it, on each side (`ruleStretches`), and no line runs along a rule (lanes sit at least 16 from it).

- **Places** (`layoutBands`, `columnsOf`, `settle`; Edo, 16 Nov 2026: "I'd want, ideally, one long vertical sequence… the binomial distribution… vertically aligned with the three nodes in arc one on the left column"): each topic's row is its chapter's first row plus the longest chain of its own chapter's needs above it, so every chapter starts below the last one ends. Columns are one grid through every chapter, 232 wide with 48 between, so a line in the gutter runs 24 from each card. Row by row, a topic takes the column of the prerequisite it follows (its nearest above, one in its own chapter first); where several follow one card, the one with the longest chain under it keeps the column and the rest take the nearest free ones, towards their other prerequisites; a topic that follows nothing drawn takes the leftmost free column. Then each row tries its moves (a topic not under the card it follows to every free column, every two topics swapped) and keeps one only when the drawing costs less: 10 a crossing, 5 a stretch two trunks share, 2 a topic under none of its prerequisites (1 under another than the one it follows), 1 a column. Probability's Conditional probability, Law of total probability, Bayes' rule, Random variables and distributions, Binomial distribution and Expectation and linearity run down one column.
- **Lines: one trunk each** (`routeTrunks`, `bands-routes.ts`; Edo: "There should be a single outflow of edges from that node… They should all be flowing downwards and then only branch out at the bottom"): a prerequisite's lines leave its card as one line down, part only in the gap just above a row of its topics (a bracket on one lane, dropping into each of them), and run on down to the next row it feeds. Where a card stands in the trunk's way, the trunk steps aside in the gap above it, to a free column or a gutter, nearest first and towards the topics still below; of every way to step aside and every order of the lanes in a gap, the one that crosses the fewest lines wins (a stretch two trunks would share counts half). Each gap is as deep as its lanes need, 24 apart, 48 at the least. Canvas tree's own layout and lines are untouched.
- Label: while the margin is out of view (the camera frames "You are here"), a chapter's number and name stay 64 in from the visible left edge, clear of the map's edge fade, on Home as the camera moves and on the course page as the pane scrolls (`../frame-left.ts`), in its strip, as a sticky section header does, so every chapter on screen is named; the arc he is in reads in the foreground ink.
- Margin: wide enough for the widest name whole (7px a character at 12px, plus 60 for the number and padding), and 32 before the cards.
- Header strip: 56 above every chapter's first cards, under the lanes of the lines leaving the chapter before: the hairline at its top (white 10%; 20% while the chapter holds the topic in focus, none over the first), the number and name 8 under it, on the page's surface, over any line passing through. No card sits in the strip, so the name never covers one.
- A course whose needs run from a later arc to an earlier one draws as Canvas tree.
- Measured on the demo courses (16 Nov 2026, round 14): lines crossing a chapter's rule 5 on Linear Algebra and 9 on Calculus (22 and 25 before), each with the rule stopping short.
- Measured again (round 14, second pass; `tests/map-bands.test.ts`): every prerequisite with several lines leaves its card as one trunk, 10 of 10 across Probability, Calculus, Linear Algebra and Real Analysis (7 of 10 before: Random variables left in 3 strands, Calculus's The derivative in 3 and Fundamental theorem in 4); crossings 0 on every course (0 before); no rule touches a line (0 before); Probability 1146×1072, Calculus 1377×1880, Linear Algebra 866×2108 (1238×1048, 1445×1880, 958×2084 before). Binomial distribution and Random variables sat 256 and 676 right of Conditional probability's column; now in it.

Trade-offs: the tallest of the three, and the widest on Calculus; lines from early chapters run long through later ones.

# Topic map

One course as a map on a study home: its topics in layers, prerequisites above the topics that need them, each topic saying where he stands and whether it needs him, and opening its page. Home draws it (`home-map.tsx`): Edo picked it over the old drawing and its popover on 13 Nov 2026, so it has no switch. The old drawing, its popover and its `/knowledge` page are deleted: `/knowledge` redirects to Home. `topic-map.tsx` (`data-slot` `course-map-graph`), `topic-node.tsx` (`course-map-node`, `course-map-current-tag`, `topic-need`, `topic-standing`), `topic-glyph.tsx` (`topic-glyph`, the shared pie), `need-edge.tsx` (`course-map-need`), `topic-map-layout.ts` (geometry, camera, keyboard walk), `lit-camera.ts` (the camera following `framed`) and `topic-standing.ts` (what a node says). Data: the engine's `CourseMap` (`src/core/contracts/course-map.ts`).

Every word and mark on a node must pass one test: would he understand it at a glance after several days away, without remembering the system's internals, and does it help him see where he stands or what to study? Engine level names (assisted, retained), "due" on its own, "weak" and counts of reviews fail it.

## Renderer and layout

| Part | Notes |
|---|---|
| renderer | React Flow (`@xyflow/react` 12, MIT): nodes are the app's own components, the camera pans, zooms and is bounded, and there is no scrollbar to hide. Read-only: nothing drags, connects, selects or deletes. Its stylesheet is `@xyflow/react/dist/base.css`, imported in `app/layout.tsx`; no `colorMode`, since a `dark` class on its wrapper would restate the old theme's tokens inside the map |
| layout | d3-dag 1.3.0, Sugiyama: simplex layering, optimal decrossing up to 25 topics and two-layer above, simplex coordinates. It tied ELK on crossings on the seeded courses and is a tenth of its weight; dagre crossed more (`research/packages.md`, the bake-off) |
| attribution | hidden (`proOptions.hideAttribution`). The MIT licence does not ask for the badge; the maintainers ask organisations that hide it to subscribe to React Flow Pro. Edo decides before this ships to anyone else |
| room | 48 across and 32 down around the drawing unless the frame gives its own (`margin`: Home's fade, 64 and 40), plus 20 above and below for the current topic's tag. The camera opens inside it; he can pan one topic past it (`slackOf`) and no further |

| line | 11/14 muted, on the title's left edge, 4 under it: the sidebar's fact for the topic when it needs him (`cardLine` over `sidebarSays`, `../../unit-inbox/sidebar-says.ts`), led by the icon the sidebar and the course page use for it (`FACT_ICON`: a question for Your turn, a spinner for Working, circle-x for a slip, history for a review, bookmark for a task, an arrow for Today's plan), else where he stands in words (`standing.words`). The one rule: a card says exactly what the sidebar's row (or Today's card on top of it) says for the topic, unless that only says when its review comes back ("Back in 5d", "Due since 21 Oct") or when he last studied it ("3d ago"), or the sidebar has no row for it; then it says where he stands. So a topic whose session waits on him says Your turn here as on the sidebar and the course page, never its slip (Linear Algebra's Rank said "Mistake to fix" while both said Your turn, 14 Nov 2026), and a topic still being learned says its review only when today's plan takes it up, as the sidebar does. Every map gets `said` from its page: Home's map and the enlarged map from Home's read, the course page's from its model (`CourseView.said`), the topic page's map of what it builds on from the topic's facts |

| need | a 1px white 33% line (3:1 on the page, WCAG 1.4.11) from the prerequisite's bottom centre to the topic's top centre: one S-curve between layers that leaves and arrives vertically, and straight down between a layer's topics where it skips that layer, so it never crosses a topic. No arrowheads: down is the direction |

Not on a node: the open task's title (on the topic's page), "Needs" (the lines say it, and a screen reader hears it as the node's description), "Contrasts with" (said only when he mixed the two up) and the engine's stale flag.

What the ring card fixed (Edo, 13 Nov 2026: the node "does not feel like a clean, polished final state"): titles in one layer share a baseline, since every card has the same two lines; the pie sits on both lines' middle instead of hanging from the first; one surface for every topic in place of two; and the line speaks Home's words.

## Behaviour

- A click, or Enter on the focused node, opens the topic's page (`/unit/<course>/<topic>`, `unit-inbox.md`). No popover and no double-click. A drag that starts on a node pans and opens nothing.
- The map opens on the whole course, centred, when it fits the frame at 80% or more (`READABLE_ZOOM`: the 13px titles draw at 10.4px, about the 11px under which a title is too small; 75% drew 9.75px, the round-3 evaluation); else framed on the current topic (`framedCamera`): the current topic and its tag clear of the fade, then the most topics it leads to whole, then around it as many whole topics as the frame holds, the topics it leads to first, then its prerequisites, at full size or down to 80% when a smaller scale shows more. A card cut at the frame's edge elsewhere does not count (Edo, 14 Nov 2026: a cut card is fine, the vignette says the map goes on; only "You are here" and its next topics must be whole, and a map is never shrunk or moved just to avoid a cut). Ranking cuts first had kept Poisson off Probability's course page and opened Linear Algebra on Home looking past its last layer, an empty band above Today (round 5). On an axis the course overflows, the opening view never looks past the drawing's room, so the frame never ends on an empty band. A card is cut when part of it shows, even faintly in the fade, and the frame's edge cuts the rest (`cardFit`); a card is whole when it is wholly in the frame (clear, or partly under the fade). The room around the drawing is in the frame's pixels at every scale, since the fade is drawn on the screen: scaling it with the drawing put the tag of a topic on the first layer into the fade below 100%, so the course page could only open at full size. Edo's 14" window, on the demo seed (`tests/topic-map-framing.test.ts`): every course opens on "You are here" with every topic it leads to whole, except Probability's course page, whose 696px pane cannot hold Random variables' four next topics in a row at 80% (they need 851px): it opens on Binomial, Geometric and Expectation whole, Poisson past the pane's edge. It returns to that view whenever the frame grows or shrinks. A page that scrolls the map itself (the course page) passes `zoom` and gets the whole course at that scale in the frame's middle; `whole` shows the whole course at whatever scale fits it (the enlarged map's Whole course), and `onOpensWhole` tells the page whether there is more to see than the opening view.
- Whole course: a page whose map opens on less than the whole course offers the maps' one zoom out control (`whole-course-button.tsx`, `course-map-whole`): a quiet button, a Zoom out icon and the words "Whole course", which shows every topic and becomes "Back to where you are" with a Locate icon; `-` toggles it while it shows, unless a field has the key. The page holds the choice for the visit only. Home's enlarged map passes it as `whole`; the course page resizes its surface to the pane instead (split-course.md).
- Drag or scroll pans; a pinch (or Ctrl and scroll) zooms between the whole course, however small, and full size.
- Hover or focus lights a topic with its whole prerequisite chain: their lines rise to white 70% and every other topic drops to 50% (120ms); the other lines drop to 35%. Edo asked for the unrelated cards dimmer than 60% (14 Nov 2026) so the chain stands out; at 50% a dimmed card's title still reads, its muted line falls just under 3:1 (at 35% it read at 1.9:1, the round-2 evaluation).
- `highlight` (optional, off by default): a set of topics a page lights from elsewhere, drawn as a hover draws a chain (the set's nodes and the lines between them lit, the other topics at 50%). A hover or focus takes over while it lasts and the highlight returns after it. The course page points at a topic's chain this way; Home lights its line's topics.
- `framed` (optional, off by default): topics a page points at, which the camera brings into view (`lit-camera.ts`). It is the course page's pane glide (split-course.md, "Pointing") on the map's own camera: once the page rests on them for 120ms, when they are not all clear of the fade, it glides in 200ms to frame them whole, as the course page's `scrollOn` brings a row's topic in, cutting as few cards at the frame's edge as it can and moving as little as it can, at its scale or as far out as the topics need to fit; "You are here" and its tag stay clear too when they fit with them. Once the page points at nothing for 400ms, or at topics this drawing does not hold, it glides back to the opening view. A pan, scroll or zoom of his own, or a keyboard walk, leaves the camera where he left it until the next point; after that point, leaving returns it as usual. Under reduced motion it moves at once. Off while the whole course shows. Home passes the topics of the Today row he points at (home-map.md).
- `marked` (optional, off by default): the one topic a page points at from elsewhere, so he sees at once which card it is (Edo, 14 Nov 2026). Its card wears `lh-card-mark` (`app/globals.css`): its border in the accent, a 1px accent ring and a soft 16px glow, held while it is marked; it blooms in once over 1.6s, a 2px ring and a 28px glow for the first second, then settles, with no loop, and fades out over 150ms when the mark leaves. Under reduced motion the settled ring shows at once. The course page marks the topic of the row he points at or arrows to.
- Keyboard: one tab stop, the current topic. Up goes to the nearest prerequisite, Down to the nearest topic that needs it, Left and Right along the layer; a topic reached out of view is brought into it (200ms). The focused node draws a 2px `ring` ring 2px outside its border (`focus-visible:ring-2`, offset on the background): an outline would never show, since the app's no-ring law in `globals.css` removes every outline. The drawing is a `figure` whose caption names the course, its topic count and where he is; each node is a link named by its title, "You are here", its standing in words and its line, and described by its prerequisites.
- Motion: none on open. The map is hidden only until its frame is measured, and its camera is placed before that frame paints, so every node shows at once, already framed (the round-3 evaluation saw nodes fade in 2.5s late, edges first). Zooming out to the whole course and back glides over 200ms; under reduced motion the camera moves at once.

## Arcs

`arc` (optional) draws one arc of a course with arcs (`arc-view.ts`, `arcView`; design-v2.md 5.3): the arc's topics whole, and its entry topics (what it builds on from earlier arcs, the engine's `entry`) in the top layer (`layoutTopicMap`'s `top`), each card at 60% with its line "From <arc>" under a corner-up mark (`topic-from`, `data-entry`). Only the needs between drawn topics are drawn, none between two entry topics, and none on an entry topic that a longer path in the drawing already carries, so an entry card never pulls a topic away from the arc it builds on. Hover lights a chain inside the drawing only; an entry card lights at full. Each arc of the seeds stays far under the 25-topic optimal decrossing limit.

The arc strip (`../arc-strip.tsx`, `ArcStrip`) names a course's arcs above its map as pills (shadcn's ToggleGroup, its `pill` variant, toggle-group.md), each its ring and its name, no counts, the shown one on the selected fill: bubbles, never underline tabs, so they never read as Home's course tabs (Edo, 14 Nov 2026). `ArcPill` is that pill on its own: the only way an arc is named anywhere (the course head, What next, the topic head), never as "Course · Arc" text.

# Calendar

`/calendar`: when and what is scheduled, across every course, by day. Reached from the sidebar's Calendar row, under Home. `data-slot="calendar"`. While it reads, or with the engine down, it says so in the page's notice, as every page (`PageNotice`, `../page-frame/page-frame.md`, "Notices").

## Data

The engine owns every date; the page invents none (`calendar-facts.ts`, pure, tested in `tests/calendar-facts.test.ts`).

| Entry | Day | From |
|---|---|---|
| Exam | the record's exam date, the session he aims at when it lists several | `map`'s `exam`; its panel says the next session under the day when the exam lists one after it ("Next session: Wednesday 3 February", `nextSessionWords`), then, from the same daily plan the calendar draws (read up to the exam however far off), the topics it has no room to start (`plan.does_not_fit`) or that every topic is started, then the road to it: each week's new topics before the exam, and the day the final review starts (`plan.final_review_days` before the exam), or that it is under way, among the weeks by date (`roadLines`; round 9 listed a start inside it above it), with what it leaves out and why in the head's words (`finalReviewGapWords`: "No room in it for Kernel", "Poisson starts too close to the exam for a review") |
| Overdue, Review | today for every review `plan` makes today (Overdue when past their date), `due`'s order first, then the rest its day today holds, since `due` names only five (17 Nov: a sixth's first entry was its next review after the exam); a later day's reviews as the engine's daily plan proposes them | `plan`'s `due`; `plan.plan.days`' `reviews` |
| New topic | the day the daily plan proposes to start it, today included | `plan.plan.days`' `new` |
| Open work: Home's status for it ("Your turn", "Left off today", "Left off 6 Nov"), else Unfinished | today, since the engine dates no task (an open task) | `map`'s `tasks`; the words are Home's and the sidebar's (`nowLine`'s status; round 12, 2 n2: Problem sheet 4 read "Left off today" on Home and "Unfinished" here) |
| Lesson, Practice | the day of the session's last message (a day after the engine's counts as today) | the session store: practice, else a lesson, whatever step it reached (a lesson still asking its opening questions is not a quiz, round 3) |
| Continue, for a lesson or practice left today | the course's next study day the plan holds, unless the plan starts its topics later | the session store: every session the session state reads as left (`sessionState`), but a review, which rides with its topics' next review. The one entry of the left work, Home's title ("Continue Stirling's formula lesson", "Continue Rank practice") and the day it was left as that day reads it ("Left off 10 Nov", `leftWords`), never "Left off today" on a later day; today's entry is then what he did, Lesson or Practice (round 19, m6: one lesson read "Left off today" on Tue 10 and again on Wed 11; round 20, m3: Rank practice, whose close said it continues Monday, was missing from Mon 9) |
| Continue, for work Home's Today lists from an earlier day | today | Home's Today (`needsYouNow`): each session line no entry of today holds yet (an open task's holder or the session its Continue opens), in Home's title and status ("Continue Eigenvalues and eigenvectors lesson · Left off 10 Nov"), opening that session; its own day's entry is then what he did (round 21, m6: 17 Nov listed neither Home's Eigen lesson nor its Rank practice) |

One review is one entry: an open task, or a session of today, that holds only topics in today's review is that review, so it folds into the review's entry, whose action opens it (round 3 saw one review as Overdue, Unfinished and Quiz). One lesson is one entry too: an open task that an unfinished session of today worked on (every topic the task holds, or the task saved by the session) folds into that session's entry, which keeps the session's name and status, the ones the sidebar and Home give it, and opens the lesson (round 4 saw one lesson as "Eigenvalues lesson: eigenspaces and diagonalisation" and "Eigenvalues and eigenvectors").

A week's day head says Day off on a weekday the engine has learned he does not study (`plan`'s `capacity.study_days`), else the Journal's study windows for that day (`journal.upcoming_schedule`, read by `src/server/calendar.ts`). The one clock is the engine's study day.

The daily plan is the engine's projection (`learning/agenda.py`): every course together, each day holding what he gets through on that weekday as his record shows, nothing on a day off, paced to each exam, assuming each planned review succeeds. A stored review date the plan does not propose that day (one that did not fit) is not drawn. Not in the engine, so not on the page: a date for an open task, and practice on a date.

Order within a day: the exam, a session that waits on him, overdue reviews, new topics (the day's lesson, so a busy month day never folds it under More), reviews, open tasks, then sessions. A new topic's panel says when it is planned and offers the topic page's Start.

## Actions

A session opens `/chat/<id>`. A review or open task opens its panel: the action starts the session the topic page would start (its first message, linked to the topic), Open topic opens `/unit/<course>/<topic>`. A month's date opens its week. The course filter keeps one course and drops sessions outside a course.

# Resume

A topic is a way back in. On Learning and Review the row (`data-testid="unit-row"`) has two lines: the title with its one status, then the session a click resumes (its title, or Lesson or Practice when the title is the topic's; the course leads when every course is listed). A topic with no session reads Study this in the accent, and a click starts one. On hover or focus the status gives way to the session count, a menu of every session newest first and Study this. On Known a row is one 32px line with its return, and a click resumes or starts the same way.

Height: 44px rows, 32px known rows.

Sources: T3 Code's hover-swapped status slot (`SidebarThreadRow.tsx`, the slot keeps its width), Gmail's conversation rows (the thread opens on its latest message), Duolingo's path (the main action is continue).

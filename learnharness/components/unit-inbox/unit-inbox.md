# Topic sidebar and topic page

The sidebar (`unit-sidebar.tsx`; Edo picked it on 13 Nov 2026 over the sessions grouped by course, deleted with the Sidebar switch) is T3 Code's sidebar with course topics in place of threads. A row is a topic the learner has started; one click opens its page (`unit-page.tsx`, `/unit/<course>/<topic>`). Sessions are never rows under a topic: they live on the topic's page.

## Sections

| Section             | T3 Code's | A topic is in it when                                                                                                                        | Order                                                                                                                         | Row                 |
| ------------------- | --------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------- |
| the list, no header | Active    | taught and not yet solid (`introduced`, `attempted`, `assisted`), a session on it waits on the learner, or it is solid and its review is due | waits on the learner, then being learned (the latest studied first), then due reviews (weak first, then the longest past due) | card                |
| Working             | Working   | the tutor is writing in one of its sessions                                                                                                  | the latest studied first                                                                                                      | card                |
| Known               | Snoozed   | solid (`independent` and up) and not due: on the engine's timer, back on the list when its review falls due                                  | the soonest back first                                                                                                        | one line            |
| Other sessions      | Settled   | sessions tied to no listed topic: a general question, or older than the course rule                                                          | newest first                                                                                                                  | one line, a session |

The list draws its top as Home's Today ("Today on top", below); these sections are how `units.ts` (`buildInbox`) classifies every topic, which the shelves under Today read (`now-shelves.md`). It is the one classifier; the engine owns every level and date. A topic not started (`new`) is not listed: the tutor routes there. The shelves sit at the foot (a spacer with `mt-auto`, so on a short list they rest at the bottom), start folded, and show 10 rows, then "Show more", or "Show the rest" when one press shows them all (`moreWords`). A folded shelf still shows the open row, and Other shows a 6px accent dot when a hidden session waits on the learner. Folding persists in `lh:topic-sidebar-folded`, the course filter in `lh:unit-inbox-scope`.

## Width

The right edge resizes the sidebar, as T3 Code's rail does (`sidebar-width.ts`, `sidebar-resize-handle.tsx`, `data-slot="sidebar-resize"`).

| | Value | T3 Code's |
|---|---|---|
| default | 256px (16rem) | `THREAD_SIDEBAR_DEFAULT_WIDTH = 16 * 16` (`threadSidebarWidth.ts`) |
| minimum | 208px (13rem) | `THREAD_SIDEBAR_MIN_WIDTH = 13 * 16` |
| maximum | the window less 640px for the page, never under the minimum | `THREAD_MAIN_CONTENT_MIN_WIDTH = 40 * 16`, `resolveThreadSidebarMaximumWidth` |
| handle | 16px wide, centred on the border, from under the 52px window band to the foot; a 2px line shows on hover, focus and drag | `SidebarRail`: `w-4`, `after:w-[2px]`, `hover:after:bg-sidebar-border` (`ui/sidebar.tsx`) |
| cursor | `w-resize` on the handle, `col-resize` on the body while dragging, with text selection off | `SidebarRail` `cursor-w-resize`; `useResizeDrag` sets `col-resize` and `user-select: none` |
| drag | pointer capture; the width is written straight to the sidebar each frame with its slide off, and stored once the pointer lets go; leaving the window commits where the pointer was; closing the sidebar mid-drag drops the drag | `useResizeDrag` (`requestAnimationFrame`, blur commits, unmount cancels) |
| double-click | back to 256px, the stored width forgotten | `AppSidebarLayout` `SidebarRail onDoubleClick={resetSidebarWidth}` |
| kept | `localStorage` `lh:sidebar-width`, narrowed to fit a smaller window | `chat_thread_sidebar_width`, `resolveInitialThreadSidebarWidth` |
| keyboard | the handle is a focusable `separator` (vertical, with its value, minimum and maximum, controlling the sidebar): ← and → move it 16px, Home to the minimum, End to the maximum; the tooltip says "Drag to resize" | T3 Code's rail has none; WAI-ARIA's window splitter pattern |

The open and close slide, the window's drag band and the Home row with its close button are as before; the handle exists only while the sidebar is open.

## Folded

Closing the sidebar (its button, ⌘B) leaves a rail (`sidebar-rail.tsx`, `data-slot="sidebar-rail"`), as T3 Code's and shadcn's collapsible icon sidebars do: 48px wide (in the Mac app as wide as the traffic lights, under their 52px band, `.lh-rail`), with Open sidebar on top, a hairline, then Home, Calendar, Search (opens the sidebar on its field) and New lesson as 28px icon buttons, and Settings at the foot. Each is named in a tooltip on its right with its shortcut; Home and Calendar hold the selected fill on their page. The pages' own open button is gone: the rail holds it. shadcn's `Sidebar` itself is not used: its fixed width and provider would replace the resizable sidebar and its Today list rather than fold them.

## Keyboard

Every row is a Tab stop; `sidebar-tree.ts` moves focus without opening: ↑/↓ step through the rows, Home and End jump to the ends, → opens a folded shelf (or steps into an open one), and on a row with an action (Resume, Study, Review) moves to the action, which shows with its ring; ← folds a shelf, goes back from an action to its row, or from a shelf row to its header. Enter opens; Space opens a topic. ↓ from an empty search goes to the first row; with a query, ↑/↓ walk the matching sessions and Enter opens one.

## Search

Topics and sessions whose titles match, under Topics and Sessions, in the rows' own anatomy. Matching courses come first under Courses, each a 36px shelf row (`course-result-row.tsx`, `data-slot` `course-result`: the course's mark and name) that opens the course's page (components/course-page/course-page.md); the field's label and tooltip read "Search topics, lessons and courses", the words its shortcut has in the keyboard list (`src/core/keyboard.ts`), and its placeholder "Search".

## The topic page

What a topic row or a map node opens: the head, the history sorted into Now, Next, Scheduled and Earlier, which holds the page's action, and the map of the lesson picked in it. See `topic-page/topic-page.md`. A new lesson starts from the sidebar's New lesson; the page has no composer.

## Sessions to topics

A session's topics come from its own transcript (`sessionTopicsOf` in `src/core/native-messages.ts`): the topics of each problem it posed and observation it saved, the topics a save introduced (a save sent as JSON text read as the engine reads it), and the topics it was started on. A plan step never counts, whatever its name, and an open task links its sessions to the task, not to each topic it covers. Only topics the course map lists count; a session on two topics is under both. Sessions started on a topic's page are on it from their first turn.

## Today on top

So the list's top is exactly Home's Today: `needsYouNow` (`../study-home/today.md`, "Needs you now"), the same lines in the same order with the same reasons, each drawn as the topic its line leads with (`inbox-today.md`). He picked it on 13 Nov 2026 over Topics (each course line's other topics under its card) and the list as it was, deleted with the Inbox switch.

- The non-search list of `unit-sidebar.tsx` is `today/today-list.tsx` (`TodayList`), handed `NowListProps` (`now-list-props.ts`): Today's lines under the course filter (`nowFor`), the inbox, the courses, the linked sessions, the study day (`studyDay`: the engine's, else his own date while the engine is down), whether the courses could be read, the folds and the sidebar. While the list loads it shows `NowSkeleton`.
- Its parts: `now-inbox.ts` (pure: `nowFor`, `unitFor`, `topicUnit`, `restShelves`, `shelfStatus`, `waitsOnYou`, `NOW_SHELF_WORDS`; `tests/now-inbox.test.ts`), `now-card.tsx` (a line as a card, `now-card.md`), `row-card.tsx` (the card shell `TopicCard` shares, `row-card.md`), `now-shelves.tsx` with `now-skeleton.tsx` and `sidebar-note.tsx` (`now-shelves.md`), `continue-course.ts` (`useContinueCourse`, Home's Continue from the sidebar) and `WhyMark` at size `slot` (`../study-home/agenda-rows/why-mark.md`). `TopicSlimRow` takes an optional `status` and `action`; without them it is as above.
- The course filter keeps only the chosen course's lines: its course line and the sessions in it.

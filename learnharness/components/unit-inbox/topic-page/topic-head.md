# Topic head

`TopicHead`, the topic page's head: the course page's head's twin (`PageHead` `compact`, `../../page-frame/page-frame.md`; Edo picked Road as the course page on 17 Nov 2026), so both pages share the title's place and weight, level with the rail's open button with no line above it, and the two pages' columns start at the same height. Props: `{ facts, children? }` (`children`: lines under the mistake, the start's error). It has no button: the page's action is Now in the history pane (`pickup/pickup.md`).

| Line | Content | Style |
|---|---|---|
| Title | `facts.topic.title` through `MathText` | `page-title`, the course page's 20/500, row min 32px, the head's first row |
| Place and standing | one line: where the topic sits on the app's one trail (`Crumbs`, `../../nav/crumbs.md`, the lesson's top bar's): the course (`courseHref`), then in a course with arcs a chevron and the arc's label (`ArcLabel`, its number then its name, `arcHref`, truncating), each opening its place on the course page, never a pill or a ring (beside the topic's pie it would read as a second standing); then `PageFact`s: the app's one pie (`../../ui/pie.md`, `TopicGlyph`, the one Home's rows and the map's cards draw) leading `facts.standing.words`, which follow the pie's fill ("Right with help" by a three-quarter pie, never "On your own", and "Slipping" by a pie in the warning ink; `components/study-words.ts`), then each of `facts.marks` (slip: its own icon, `slip-icons.ts`, review: `HistoryIcon`, the map's and Today's icons; the sidebar keeps the slip as the row's reason). The place shows alone when the standing is silent (a topic not started that a session only touched) | `page-standing`, `text-row` muted, the trail's crumbs as `Crumbs` draws them; each fact `page-fact` with `data-kind` (`standing`, `slip`, `review`), a 16px mark 6px from its words, 16 between facts, wrapping under; a hidden ". " before each fact so a screen reader pauses after the place |
| Mistake | `facts.mistake`: the engine's diagnosis, else a weak topic's last attempt that was not right, in the tutor's words, through `MathText` (a formula typesets once the tutor writes it as `$…$`) | `data-slot="unit-gap"`, `text-row` at 90% ink |

One left edge, the column's, for every line (Edo, 14 Nov 2026: the head "looks so messy and unstructured": the pie hung on its own edge, the words and the mistake on a second one 32px in).

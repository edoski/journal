# Topic head

`TopicHead`, the topic page's head: the shared page head (`PageHead`, `../../page-frame/page-frame.md`), the course page's, so both pages share the title's place and weight and the standing line's facts. Props: `{ facts, children? }` (`children`: lines under the mistake, the start's error). It has no button: the page's action is Now in the history pane (`pickup/pickup.md`).

| Line | Content | Style |
|---|---|---|
| Course | `eyebrow`: `facts.course.title`, a link to the course's page (`courseHref`, `data-slot="topic-course-link"`) | `page-eyebrow`, `text-label` muted, full ink on hover, 2px focus ring |
| Title | `facts.topic.title` through `MathText` | `page-title`, the course page's 20/500, row min 32px |
| Standing | `PageFact`s: the app's one pie (`../../ui/pie.md`, `TopicGlyph`, the one Home's rows and the map's cards draw) leading `facts.standing.words`, which follow the pie's fill ("Right with help" by a three-quarter pie, never "On your own", and "Slipping" by a pie in the warning ink; `components/study-words.ts`), then each of `facts.marks` (slip: its own icon, `slip-icons.ts`, review: `HistoryIcon`, the map's and Today's icons; the sidebar keeps the slip as the row's reason), then `facts.studied` (`ClockIcon`, the course page's), then in a course with arcs the topic's arc as a bubble (`ArcPill` at `segment-sm`, `../../course-map/arc-pill.md`: its ring, `facts.arc.share`, and its name, one line), which opens the course page on that arc (`arcHref`); the row shows the bubble alone when the standing is silent | `page-standing`, `text-row` muted; each fact `page-fact` with `data-kind` (`standing`, `slip`, `review`, `studied`), a 16px mark 6px from its words, 16 between facts, wrapping under; a hidden ". " before each fact after the first so a screen reader pauses. Absent when `facts.standing` is null (a topic not started that has a session) and the course has no arcs. The hybrid passes no review mark and no studied day: Now, Scheduled and Earlier say them |
| Mistake | `facts.mistake`: the engine's diagnosis, else a weak topic's last attempt that was not right, in the tutor's words, through `MathText` (a formula typesets once the tutor writes it as `$…$`) | `data-slot="unit-gap"`, `text-row` at 90% ink |

One left edge, the column's, for every line (Edo, 14 Nov 2026: the head "looks so messy and unstructured": the pie hung on its own edge, the words and the mistake on a second one 32px in).

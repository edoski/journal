For the current lab, work in **discrete time**: signals and the impulse response are indexed by integers, so write $g[n]$ rather than $g(t)$. That changes operations such as convolution from integrals to sums. Use **Lumen** for the current conventions and **Atlas** when you want a fuller derivation, translating its notation and time assumptions as needed.
